    How to move judges

    Drag a judge onto another row to share its rank, or into the lane between two rows to give them a rank of their own.

    ↑ ↓
    Move past the judge above or below
    ⇧ + ↑ ↓
    Split into a new tier
    ⌥ + ↑ ↓
    Join the tier above or below
    F
    Flag a judge you mean to move

    Untouched tiers keep their original rank, gaps and all, and a move takes a free rank number so nobody else is renumbered unless there is no room.

      Drag is off while a filter is active — the tiers you'd drop into are hidden. Arrow keys still work, and swap with the nearest judge still showing.
